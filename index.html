About me

I build, test and learn through practice.

Hello! My name is Dominik, I'm 22 years old and I'm from Poland. I'm passionate about creating websites, applications and meaningful solutions. I love learning new technologies and sharing knowledge.

My goal is to become a programmer, and that's the point: I'm still discovering myself in this colorful area. It is fascinating to discover IT from scratch using C, C++, creating your own electronic circuits, but also finding out what the latest programming modules for languages like Python, JavaScript or PHP have to offer.

That's why I chose IT studies with a programming specialty, where I discover both worlds of high and low level coding. On this website you can find my projects, latest ideas, thoughts and contact info.
